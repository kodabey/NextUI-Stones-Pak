STONES - Go for TrimUI Brick / Brick Hammer (NextUI)

Install: copy Stones.pakz to the root of the SD card and boot NextUI
(it unpacks itself), or unzip Stones-sdcard.zip at the SD card root so
you get /Tools/tg5040/Stones.pak/. Launch "Stones" from Tools.

Controls
  D-pad   move cursor          A      place stone
  B       take back move       X      pass
  Y       hint (top 3 moves)   SELECT territory map / score estimate
  L1      jump to last move    START  menu (sound, atari alerts, resign, quit)

Opponents
  Pebble (石)   beginner - plays loosely, makes mistakes
  Brook (川)    casual
  Mountain (山) club player
  Dragon (龍)   strongest; thinks up to ~10 s on big boards
  A friend (友) hot-seat two-player

Rules: Chinese area scoring, komi 7.5 (0.5 with handicap), simple ko.
After both players pass, the game proposes dead stones; press A on a
group to flip it, START to confirm.

Games auto-save after every move ("Continue" on the title screen).
Finished games are written as SGF to SD:/.userdata/shared/Stones/games/
Log: SD:/.userdata/tg5040/logs/Stones.txt
